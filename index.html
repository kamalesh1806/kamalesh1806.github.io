01 / BookedOut — Paris Airbnb
Predictive Modeling + Causal Inference for Investor Strategy
PythonRLightGBMXGBoostCatBoostPSMLogistic Regression
MSBA · SPRING 2026
VIEW DETAILS

Analyzed 4,391 Paris listings (17e arrondissement) to produce an investor recommendation backed by predictive modeling and causal inference. LightGBM + XGBoost + CatBoost ensemble hit AUC 0.861 — a +10.1% lift over baseline, finishing #2 on the Kaggle leaderboard. The entire gain came from feature engineering (NA flags, availability ratios, log-price-vs-neighborhood-median), not algorithm choice. Propensity Score Matching isolated the +12.4pp causal lift of flexible cancellation, and revealed that Superhost status only hurts bookings because of pricing — three independent methods (logit, PSM, XGBoost) converged on north Batignolles, $85/night, flexible cancellation, entire apartment.

02 / NBA Expansion Optimization
ILP + Nash Bargaining Across 15 City Pairings
Integer Linear ProgrammingNash BargainingANOVAR
MSBA · SPRING 2026
VIEW DETAILS

Evaluated all 15 possible two-city pairings for the NBA's growth to 32 teams using two complementary ILP models. Validated Theory Distance — a schedule-invariant travel proxy — against 14 years of actual NBA travel data via ANOVA (F=163.3, p<0.001). Travel-only minimization picked Tampa + Montreal at 72,659 mi (32,304 mi better than worst). Nash Bargaining (travel + revenue) shifted the top pick to Montreal + Mexico City at <0.001% efficiency loss. Montreal is the universal pick — the real tradeoff is Tampa (efficiency) vs. Mexico City (market scale).

03 / Airline Tweet Sentiment
NLP Pipeline for Customer-Service Routing
PythonRTF-IDFXGBoostLassoLDAK-Means
MSBA · SPRING 2026
VIEW DETAILS

End-to-end NLP classifier on 14,640 airline tweets, designed around the asymmetric cost of missing a viral complaint. TF-IDF + Lasso (λ=0.01) reached F1=0.814; XGBoost at threshold 0.30 caught 99.7% of negative tweets. TF-IDF beat Bag-of-Words on recall (0.973 vs 0.825) by suppressing universal terms like "flight". LDA (k=3) split complaints into Customer Service, Cancellations, and Baggage themes, with K-Means then segmenting riders into operational profiles. A few-shot LLM with explicit sarcasm rules hit 100% on the 20-tweet edge-case set.

04 / Diabetes Risk Prediction
ML Risk Stratification on 253K CDC Records
PythonLogistic RegressionRandom ForestKNNSVMScikit-learn
MSBA · JAN–MAR 2026
VIEW DETAILS

Six-model classification pipeline on CDC BRFSS using 21 self-reported indicators — no lab tests required. The instructive finding: SVM topped accuracy (84.5%) but had the worst at-risk recall (8.3%) — exactly the opposite of what the clinical cost structure requires. Selected Random Forest at threshold 0.70 instead: 49.4% at-risk recall, 87.1% specificity. Top predictors were General Health, BMI, Age, High BP, High Cholesterol. Error analysis showed the 18–39 bracket has only 30% recall (vs 50% for 60+) — motivating age-stratified models as the next step.

05 / Supply Chain Optimization
19-Plant · 10-Port LP Cost Minimization Model
Excel SolverLinear ProgrammingSensitivity AnalysisShadow Prices
MSBA · JAN–MAR 2026
VIEW DETAILS

190-variable LP minimizing total landed cost across a 19-plant, 10-port network. Optimal solution: 4,057 units/day at 70% utilization, $466K total ($299K production + $167K shipping). Shadow prices revealed the network is port-constrained, not plant-constrained: PORT04 expansion adds 1,063 units/day vs PORT02's 383 — 2.8× ROI per unit of investment. The PLANT07 paradox: the cheapest plant in the network ($0.37/unit) produces zero output because its only downstream port (PORT02) is completely full. Cost-only or capacity-only analysis would have missed this entirely.

06 / VAR Monetary Policy Analysis
Vector Autoregression for Fed–Inflation–Unemployment Dynamics
PythonRVARADF/KPSSGranger CausalityIRFFEVD
MSBA · JAN–MAR 2026
VIEW DETAILS

Modeled the dynamic feedback between Federal Funds Rate, Unemployment, and Inflation on 752 monthly observations (1954–2017). Validated VAR estimation on simulated data with a known coefficient matrix first, then fit VAR(3) on first-differenced series (BIC-selected). Granger causality confirmed all three monetary channels (EFFR → unemployment, F=8.89, p=1.3e-9). FEVD revealed inflation is the most externally driven variable — at 24 months, EFFR + unemployment together explain >40% of its forecast variance. IRFs showed delayed, persistent effects consistent with known monetary transmission lags.

07 / Uber vs Ola Review Comparison
1.7M Indian Rider Reviews · Sentiment + Topic Modeling
PythonRBing LexiconLDALogistic Regression
MSBA · OCT–DEC 2025
VIEW DETAILS

Analyzed 1.7M Indian rider reviews to go beyond star ratings. Bing lexicon sentiment: Ola is 66.55% negative; Uber is 52.73% positive — the only platform with a majority positive balance. Ola reviewers also write longer reviews at every star level (dissatisfied customers elaborate). LDA (k=5) surfaced 5 complaint themes; "driver" was the #1 negative term on both platforms — driver quality is the shared pain point of the Indian ride-hailing market. Logistic regression with sentiment + length + company gave Uber an OR of ≈2.70 (p<2e-16) — the platform difference is real after fully controlling for text content.
